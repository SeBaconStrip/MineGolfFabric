# Golf Cart (package D) – design

A drivable two-seat cart with a holder for a golf bag.

## Behaviour

- **Item** `golf_cart` (stack 1). Use on a block top: spawns a cart there, facing the player's view direction.
  Recipe: 3 white wool over iron ingot, minecart, iron ingot.
- **Seats**: 2. Right-click rides; the first passenger drives (left seat), the second sits on the right.
  Passengers turn with the cart and can look up to 105° to the side, like in a boat.
- **Driving** (client of the driver is authoritative, like boats):
  - W accelerates to `MAX_SPEED` 0.35 blocks/tick (7 blocks/s).
  - S brakes; when stopped it reverses up to `MAX_REVERSE` 0.125 blocks/tick.
  - No input: the cart coasts and slows down by friction.
  - A/D steer. The turn rate grows with speed up to 5°/tick and is mirrored when reversing; no turning while standing.
  - Gravity applies; the cart climbs 1-block steps (`maxUpStep` 1.0). Hitting a wall stops it.
  - In water it sinks and passengers are ejected after 3 s under water (like boats).
- **Bag holder**:
  - Right-click with a golf bag in hand: the bag moves from the hand onto the cart (only when the holder is empty).
  - Sneak + right-click with an empty hand: the bag goes back to the player.
  - The bag is drawn on the rear deck.
  - While riding, the club key `R` takes clubs from the cart's bag when the player has no bag in the inventory.
- **Breaking**: hit it like a boat. It drops the cart item and the loaded bag. In creative it is removed without drops.

## Structure

- `physics/CartPhysics` (pure Java): `step(State, Input)` updates speed and yaw.
  - `State(speed, yaw)`; `Input(forward, back, left, right)`; yaw is in degrees.
- `entity/GolfCartEntity extends VehicleEntity`. Synced data: `HAS_BAG`. The bag stack is saved as `Bag`.
  - Driver input comes from the controlling player's `zza` / `xxa`. Those are set from the keys on the driving
    client, which is the side that moves the cart.
- `item/GolfCartItem`.
- Client: `GolfCartModel` (cubes in code, 256×256 texture), `GolfCartRenderer`, `GolfCartRenderState`
  (yaw, hurt, wheel angle, bag). Registered with `ModelLayerRegistry` and `EntityRenderers`.
- Texture: generated by `tools/gen_assets.py` from colour zones (body, seat, tyres, roof, bag, posts).

## Testing

- JUnit `CartPhysicsTest`:
  - reaches max speed and never exceeds it;
  - brakes, then reverses at most `MAX_REVERSE`;
  - coasts to a stop;
  - no turn at rest; turns while moving; reverse steering is mirrored.
- Gametests:
  - The item places a cart.
  - Bag load and unload.
  - Breaking drops the cart and the bag.
  - R takes a club from the cart's bag while riding.
- Visual check in game: model, seat height, wheels, bag on the deck.
