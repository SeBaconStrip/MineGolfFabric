# Changelog

## 0.2.0 – course tools

- Range Finder: distance, height difference and a suggested club with power for the block you look at; aim at
  the flag to measure to the cup
- Hole Sign: placed next to a tee box it shows hole number, par and length and follows Course Book changes
- Out-of-Bounds Stakes: stakes form boundary lines; a ball that stops beyond one costs a penalty stroke and is
  replayed from where it was hit. Holding a stake or the Course Book shows the lines

## 0.1.0 – first release (Minecraft 26.3)

- Clubs: Driver, Iron, Wedge, Putter with hold-to-charge swing, power bar and aim line
- Golf ball with server-side physics, dyeable
- Turf blocks: Fairway, Rough, Green, Bunker Sand; ice, water and lava behave like on a real course
- Tee Box, Cup and Flag
- Golf Bag (4 clubs + 16 balls) with `R` to cycle clubs
- Course Book: create courses with up to 18 holes and par 2–6
- Rounds for several players at once, HUD and scorecard
- Commands `/minegolf join`, `/minegolf leave`, `/minegolf pickup`
- 20 achievements in their own tab and 10 statistics
- All recipes unlock in the recipe book
- Leave a round from the Course Book or with `/minegolf leave`
- Course Book: reorder holes, delete any hole, relink a hole whose tee or cup was rebuilt
- A rolling ball climbs a one-block step if it is fast enough, so courses can follow hilly terrain
- Turf blocks count as solid ground for heightmaps (mob spawning, rain, structure placement)
